# Independent answer audit — 25 October 2026

Six folders in this archive do not rest on an official NTA or CSIR-HRDG final answer key: 2013/June
has no official key anywhere; 2011/June, 2011/December, 2012/June and 2012/December rest on
unofficial coaching-institute keys; 2013/December rests on a provisional key. All 870 answers in
those six folders were re-derived independently from the printed question text and options by a
separate model session that cloned this repository read-only, changed nothing, and consulted the
source scans listed in each `sources.md` where the printed text was ambiguous or a figure decided
the answer.

**Result: 51 reported disagreements, of which 50 stand; 13 questions were judged too damaged to
decide.** Twelve recorded answers were contradicted by something printed, computed or drawn in the
same question block and have been corrected. No folder exceeded fifteen disagreements, so no folder
is treated as a transcription failure.

## How each disagreement was treated

1. **Key governs.** Where a genuine NTA or CSIR-HRDG key exists — including the provisional key for
   2013/December — the recorded answer stands even when independent reasoning prefers another
   option. The doubt is noted in that folder's `sources.md`.
2. **Self-contradiction is a defect.** Where the recorded answer conflicts with the folder's own
   figure description, its own worked arithmetic, or the printed option text, the answer is
   corrected to match the evidence, and the change is logged in that folder's `sources.md`.
3. **Unresolved stays visible.** Where an unofficial key and independent reasoning disagree without
   decisive evidence either way, the published answer is kept and both positions are recorded here
   and in `sources.md`. Nothing is silently switched.
