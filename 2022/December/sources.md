## 3. Known conflicts

- **Question 65 (Part B; printed Client Question ID 703065)** — "Which one of the
  following types of promoters would **NOT** be used within the T-DNA for expression
  of a negative selection marker gene for generation of transgenic plants by
  *Agrobacterium*-mediated transformation?" Options: 1. strong constitutive
  promoter; 2. tissue-specific promoter; 3. substrate-inducible promoter; 4.
  stress-inducible promoter.
  - **NTA Final Answer Key marks option 1 (strong constitutive promoter).** The
    Part file records this as the answer.
  - **Coaching-institute solution (Pathfinder) reads this item as option 2
    (tissue-specific promoter)** — an alternate wording/interpretation of the
    counter-selection strategy. The archive keeps the NTA verdict; the institute
    reading is noted here and inside the PartB.md Q65 explanation as
    `wording/answer conflict`.

No other wording or answer conflicts were left unresolved. Every other Part A/B/C
answer matches both official key copies.
