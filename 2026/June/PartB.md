### Question 23

*Question ID: 458908873*

Which of the following statement is INCORRECT according to Charles Darwin's theory of Natural Selection?

1. Organisms produce a far greater number of progeny than ever survived to adulthood.
2. The number of individuals in species remains more or less constant.
3. The individuals in a species are not all identical but show variation in heritable characters.
4. Parents for the next generation are randomly selected from the current generation.

**Answer: 4 (Parents for the next generation are randomly selected from the current generation)**

> **Explanation:** Darwin's theory hinges on **non-random** differential survival and reproduction of variants; the claim that parents for the next generation are 'randomly selected' contradicts natural selection, so option 4 is the incorrect statement.

### Question 24

*Question ID: 458908874*

The frequency of affected individuals for a disease caused by a recessive mutation, in an isolated population is, 1/1100. Assuming Hardy Weinberg equilibrium, what is the frequency of individuals who are carrier of this disease in the same population?

1. 0.03
2. 0.06
3. 0.97
4. 0.0009

**Answer: 2 (0.06)**

> **Explanation:** For a recessive disease, affected frequency $q^2 = 1/1100 \Rightarrow q \approx 0.030$. Carrier frequency $2pq \approx 2q = 0.060$, i.e. **0.06**.

C. Dictyoglomi / III. Intracellular pathogen
D. Planctomycetes / IV. Green non-sulfur
Choose the correct answer from the options given below:

1. A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I
2. A-III, B-I, C-II, D-IV
3. A-IV, B-II, C-III, D-I
4. A-II, B-III, C-I, D-IV

**Answer: 1 (A-III, B-IV, C-II, D-I)**

> **Explanation:** Chlamydiae are obligate intracellular pathogens (A-III); Chloroflexi are the green non-sulfur bacteria (B-IV); Dictyoglomi are thermophilic/heat-resistant (C-II); Planctomycetes are aquatic budding bacteria (D-I).

Methylene blue and crystal violet are examples of:

1. Basic dyes
2. Basic and acidic dyes, respectively
3. Acidic and basic dyes, respectively

**Answer: 1 (Basic dyes)**

> **Explanation:** Methylene blue and crystal violet are both cationic chromophores - **basic dyes**.
