# CSIR NET Life Science — December 2020 session: archive report

## Verdict

**No Joint CSIR-UGC NET (Life Sciences) "December 2020" examination was ever conducted.**
There is therefore no December 2020 question paper, no response sheet, and no answer key
to archive. This folder deliberately contains **zero questions** rather than invented ones.

The NTA planned one (Careers360 lists the December 2020 CSIR NET date as *tentative*,
15 December 2020), but it never took place. CSIR NET then ran on a disrupted/annual cadence:
**Dec 2019 → June 2020 (held 19/21/26/30 Nov 2020) → June 2021 (held 29 Jan, 5–6 Feb and
15–17 Feb 2022)**. The December 2020 and December 2021 CSIR cycles were both skipped.

## Discrepancies found and how they were resolved

1. **Triyambak lists "December_Morning_Shift 2020" and "December_Evening_Shift 2020" — but no
   June 2020 at all.** Its Dec-2020 Morning Q1 is *"Suppose a² + b² = 4(a +3b−10), where a and b
   are two real numbers…"*. The official Pathfinder scan of the **21 Nov 2020 Shift 1** paper
   (page 2) shows `Question Number : 1 Question Id : 8024371191` with exactly that text and the
   same four options (a > b / a < b / a = b / nothing can be said).
   **Resolution:** Triyambak's "December 2020" is a **mislabel of the June-2020 cycle paper**
   (held 21 Nov 2020). It is not evidence of a December 2020 exam. Rejected.

2. **Pathfinder's "2021 Shift-1/Shift-2" files.** The URL names suggest a Dec-2020 session held in
   early 2021. **Resolution:** read page 1 with vision — it says *June-2021, 17-02-2022,
   CSIR_FEB22_SET1*. These are the June-2021 session. Rejected as Dec-2020.

3. **"December 2020 and June 2021 (merged cycles)" notices on nta.ac.in.** These look like proof of
   a CSIR Dec-2020 session. **Resolution:** they are **UGC-NET** notices (ugcnet.nta.nic.in), a
   different examination. The CSIR-UGC NET notices in the same archive never mention December 2020.

4. **Careers360 "Dates for December 2020 (Tentative)"** — a planned 15 Dec 2020 exam date.
   **Resolution:** tentative only; no subsequent admit-card/key/result notice exists. Planned ≠ held.

## Unresolved

- **Nothing is unresolved about the finding itself**; the conclusion is supported by the official
  CSIR HRDG archive, the NTA notice archive, the CSIR Annual Report, press reporting and three
  independent aggregators, with every apparent counter-example traced to a mislabel or to UGC-NET.
- **Open question for the requester (cannot be asked mid-run, so a choice was made):** the two
  sessions a person asking for "CSIR NET December 2020" might actually mean are
  (a) the **June-2020 cycle**, conducted **21 November 2020**, Shift 1 and Shift 2 — for which the
  official question papers *and* the official final answer key by Question ID were located and
  downloaded during this run, and
  (b) the **June-2021 cycle**, conducted **17 February 2022**, Shift 1 and Shift 2 — for which a
  90-page and an 86-page candidate response sheet were located.
  **Decision: neither was substituted into this folder.** Labelling another session's paper as
  "December 2020" would corrupt the archive and misattribute every Question ID and answer. The
  folder is left honestly empty. Say which of (a) or (b) you want and it can be archived properly.
