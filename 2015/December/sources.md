# Sources — CSIR NET Life Science, December 2015 (Booklet A)

## Answer key status: **FOUND — official CSIR final answer key**

The archive's answers are taken from the **official CSIR final answer key**, not from reasoning.

- **Source 1 (authoritative key):**
  `https://csirhrdg.res.in/SiteContent/ManagedContent/ContentFiles/20190716094620801lifekey_Dec2015.pdf`
  — "JOINT CSIR-UGC-JRF/NET DECEMBER 2015 FINAL ANSWER KEY, LIFE SCIENCE". Contains Booklet A/B/C, each in Bilingual and English. 6 pages. The **English Booklet A** table (page 2) was used for all answers, cross-checked against the Bilingual Booklet A table (page 1). Key legend printed on the page: *"change in key indicated in bold"* and *"*benefit of marks to those who attempted"*.

## Question paper sources (two independent, cross-verified)

- **Source 2 (primary transcription):**
  `https://pathfinderacademy.in/assets/downloads/csir-document/life-science-december-2015-paper.pdf`
  — Pathfinder Academy, 47-page typeset bilingual (Hindi + English) reproduction of Booklet A. Header: "2015 (II) जीव विज्ञान / प्रश्न पत्र", subject code 3, booklet code A, 200 marks, 3 h. Part A = pp.3–6, Part B = pp.7–16, Part C = pp.17–45. This is the legible source from which all English text and every figure crop were taken.

- **Source 3 (independent verification):**
  `https://upiqpbank.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/CSIR-NET-LIFE-SCIENCES-DECEMBER-2015-QUESTION-APPER-3A.pdf`
  — UPI QP Bank / Unique Publications, 46-page Booklet A scan (also 3B, 3C available). Compared page-for-page against Source 2 for Part A (Q1–20): question text, option text and figures agree verbatim. Two genuinely different typesetting/layout jobs (different watermarks, different page breaks), so treated as independent.

Both paper sources are typeset reproductions (not the raw NTA scan), and both carry the same Booklet-A question order, so the printed question number maps **1:1** to the key's `Q. No.` (1–145). 2015 papers do **not** print `Question ID:-` headers, so the mapping was established from the sequential numbering common to both sources and the key.

## Unresolved / flagged items

- **Q14 — key disagreement between the two key tables.** Bilingual Booklet A lists **2 or 4**; English Booklet A lists **2**. PartA.md records 2 as primary and notes the bilingual dual key. (Physics of rolling: top of wheel = 120 km/h forward = option 2; option 4 "120 km/h backward" is not physically correct, so the English single key is the defensible one.)
- **Q22 — key accepts multiple options:** official key = **1 or 2 or 3** (Aspartic acid / Glycine / Glutamine). Recorded as accepting all three; not collapsed to one.
- **Q35 — key marks "*":** "benefit of marks to those who attempted" (effectively dropped/all-credited). No single option invented.
- **Q55 — key accepts multiple options:** **2 or 3** (Cycadophyta / Ginkgophyta). Recorded as accepting both.
- **Q104 — key accepts multiple options:** **1 or 2 or 3**. Recorded as accepting all three.
- **Q137 — key marks "*":** all-attempted credit. No single option invented.
- **Q82 — arithmetic does not reproduce any option.** The printed data (mitotic index 3/25000, M-phase 30 min) give a cell-cycle length of 30 ÷ (3/25000) = 250,000 min ≈ 4167 h, which matches none of 76/50/42/21 hours. The official key designates **3 (42 hours)**. The answer is recorded per the key; the explanation flags the numbers as apparently misprinted.
- **Reasoning-vs-key corrections.** During self-check the following answers were found to differ from the official key when derived by reasoning, and were reset to the key's verdict: **Q46 (→3), Q47 (→4), Q69 (→1), Q82 (→3), Q100 (→1), Q109 (→4), Q111 (→2), Q117 (→2), Q128 (→1)**. The key is authoritative for all of them; the explanations note where the biology is contentious (e.g. Q46 h-/f-channel equivalence, Q128 open-ocean vs grassland turnover).
- **Figures:** all 19 crops are taken from Source 2 (Pathfinder) page renders at 150 DPI. Where a crop includes a line of the question's own stem above/below the figure, that is the same question's text (not another question). No figure was found illegible in either source.
