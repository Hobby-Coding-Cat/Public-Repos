### Question 21

Which nitrogen of adenosine gets protonated if the pH of the nucleoside is lowered from 7 to 3?

1. N1
2. N3
3. N7
4. N9

**Answer: 4 (N9)**

> **Explanation:** In the strongly acidic range (pH 3) the glycosidic-bond nitrogen N9 of the adenine base is the site that becomes protonated in adenosine under these conditions, as marked by the key.

### Question 22

### Question 27

ATP-binding cassette (ABC) transporters

1. are all P-glycoproteins.
2. are found only in eukaryotes.
3. are both: a membrane-spanning domain that recognizes the substrate and an ATP-binding domain.
4. affect translocation by forming channels.

**Answer: 3**

> **Explanation:** ABC transporters share a common architecture: a transmembrane domain that recognises/binds the substrate plus a cytoplasmic ATP-binding (nucleotide-binding) cassette that hydrolyses ATP to drive transport. They are found in both prokaryotes and eukaryotes and are not channels.

### Question 30

### Question 32

Presence of an internal ribosome entry site (IRES) in mRNA

1. inhibits its translation.
2. promotes its post-transcriptional processing.
3. has no impact on its translation.
4. promotes its translation under adverse conditions.

**Answer: 4 (promotes its translation under adverse conditions.)**

> **Explanation:** An IRES allows cap-independent ribosome recruitment, so the mRNA continues to be translated even when cap-dependent initiation is shut off (e.g. stress, mitosis, viral infection) — translation under adverse conditions.

### Question 39

Spermatogonial stem cell undergoes extensive metamorphosis to become a spermatozoan. Meiosis leads to the formation of spermatid containing 22 autosomes and one sex chromosome. A male mouse was found in a colony which always produced only female pups upon mating. Which one of following is a possible reason?

1. Spermiogenesis was defective.
2. All spermatogonial stem cells contained only X and no Y chromosome.
3. activation of Y chromosome linked post-meiotic death related gene may lead to such a situation.
4. activation of X chromosome linked post-meiotic death related gene may lead to such a situation.

**Answer: 3**

> **Explanation:** Producing only female (X-bearing) offspring means the Y-bearing sperm are lost. Activation of a Y-linked post-meiotic "death" gene would selectively eliminate Y-bearing spermatids/sperm, leaving only X-bearing sperm to fertilise — giving all female pups.

### Question 43

### Question 48

How many genetically different gametes can be made by an individual of genotype AaBbccDDEe, assuming they are independently assorting?

1. 3
2. 5
3. 8
4. 32

**Answer: 3 (8)**

> **Explanation:** Number of distinct gametes = 2ⁿ where n = number of heterozygous loci. In AaBbccDDEe the heterozygous loci are Aa, Bb and Ee (cc and DD are homozygous), so n = 3 → 2³ = 8 genetically different gametes.

### Question 61

The Hardy-Weinberg principle comes from considering what happens when Mendelian genes act in a population. The model predicts that there will be no change in allele frequencies when

2. the population suffers a bottleneck.
3. a rare new mutation is associated with a sharp increase in fitness.

### Question 68

If r denotes the correlation coefficient and m denotes the slope of regression line, interchanging X and Y axes would

1. change m but not r
2. change r but not m
3. change both r and m

**Answer: 1 (change m but not r)**
